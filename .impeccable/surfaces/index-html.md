# Two Keys page

## Mode

Persuade. A hospitality owner decides whether to request a project.

## Audience

Restaurant, lounge, and café owners (events next) with a lived-in place and a weak or rented website.

## Job

Understand the studio, believe they keep the site, and send a project request.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Two crossed keys are the offer — creative direction and build-and-care, one door — on a single printed sheet. The page refuses a split hero, a testimonial row, and any client or price the deck does not contain.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper `#F3EBDD`, warm black `#1E1414`, one brick red `#9E2B25`. Abril Fatface for display, Inter for text. Flat screen-print: hairline rules, red top-edges on paper cards, one ink panel, one brick close. The mark is two keys, ink and brick, with a slight misregistration. No shadows, no glass, no rounded app chrome.

STORY: The visitor sees the mark and the line “websites with vibe, ownership built in.”, then empty work plates, then the problem, the two services, the two people, the three things they own, five steps, and three priced doors with blanks. They fill Project Request and press “unlock the door”. Nothing is sent until a Formspree endpoint or mailto is added.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered sheet. Crossed keys large, then “Two Keys”, a short brick rule, the tagline, the two names, and a text link to the request. “our work” begins in the same view on a laptop. No sticky bar.

FORM: Brief-pinned. The hand sketch sets the order (mark, our work, what we do, Project Request, unlock the door) and the pitch deck sets the visual system. Concept-seed was not rolled; a tournament would replace a pinned deck and sketch. Seed key: brief-pinned, no roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Constraints

Prices, contact, and projects are placeholders. No invented proof. Static host. Form hookup is documented in the README.
