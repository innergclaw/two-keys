# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JS at the repository root, deployed with GitHub Pages. No build step. The brief specified this stack and the project URL `https://innergclaw.github.io/two-keys/`.

## Users

Independent owners of restaurants, lounges, and cafés (events next) whose place has a real in-room vibe and whose website is either missing, a template, or rented from a platform.

## Product Purpose

Two Keys is a one-page studio site. It explains the offer and collects a project request. Success is a visitor understanding what Two Keys does, what they keep, and how to ask for a project.

## Positioning

Two Keys is an OwnYourWeb studio: Alexis holds creative direction, Nasirr “G” Mayo holds build and care. The client owns the domain, the hosting, and the site. The monthly plan is maintenance, not rent.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from OwnYourWeb (`https://ownyourweb.xyz/`) or a direct link. The page is the whole product surface: work, offer, and a project request. Prices and a public contact line are not set yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One static page. Copy comes from the Two Keys pitch deck.
- Prices stay `$___`. The monthly content allowance stays `___`. Contact stays “contact coming soon” until the owner fills them in.
- The request form posts to a Formspree endpoint or a mailto address the owner adds. No personal email is hardcoded.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, statistics, or case studies. The four work marks read “project coming soon”.
- Collab name is Two Keys. Alexis’s last name is not stated in the deck and is not invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Two Keys. Line: “an OwnYourWeb studio”. Tagline: “websites with vibe, ownership built in.”
- People: Nasirr “G” Mayo and Alexis.
- Palette and type are binding: cream `#F3EBDD`, warm black `#1E1414`, brick red `#9E2B25`, Abril Fatface headings, Inter body.
- Mark: two crossed keys, one ink and one brick red.
- Close line: “your front door, your keys.”
- Page order follows the hand sketch: keys, our work, what we do, Project Request, “unlock the door”.

## Evidence on Hand

- Pitch copy and color tokens in the deck source (attached `two-keys-pitch` PDF and `build.js`).
- Hand sketch for section order.
- No photographs, client names, prices, or contact address were provided.

## Product Principles

- Say only what the deck says.
- Ownership is the offer: the client keeps the keys.
- Placeholders stay visible until the owner replaces them.
- The in-room vibe is the standard a site is judged against.
- Hospitality first, events next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The brief requires visible labels, contrast, alt text, and a mobile-first layout. Body text meets WCAG AA on the cream, ink, and brick surfaces. Form fields use native constraints, autocomplete, and error text.
